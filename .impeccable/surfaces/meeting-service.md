# Service entry and meeting room
Mode: Operate. Scope: Vue service login, teams, meeting list and meeting room.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A meeting is a continuing conversation with delegated checks, not a rigid decision template. Preserve document reading, but put context-bound AI requests inside the same meeting flow.
OWN-WORLD: White document, cool-gray surrounding workspace, charcoal primary action and quiet blue links. Self-hosted Pretendard, compact controls, precise rules, no decorative metrics.
STORY: Select and test the actual input, continue talking, delegate a context-aware check, return to its cited conversation, and review what the team wants to keep. Never present generated text as team agreement.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep the centered 1040px document, title and top transcription/save controls. A slim task strip opens an independently scrolling 410px AI helper beside the transcript on wide screens; mobile uses the content width and returns to the same transcript position when closed. Request timestamps link back to source utterances. The microphone selector is discoverable in the transcript heading. No call dock or blocking AI loader.
FORM: User-approved Notion document convention, code-first explicitly selected. Context and concept-seed scripts attempted; unavailable engine returned 127, so no seed key exists.
SIGNATURE: A question freezes the relevant moment, the conversation continues, and its answer returns in a separate scroll surface without moving the transcript. The answer names its automatically selected review role and separates meeting-grounded facts from general knowledge and assumptions. Updated conversation is flagged rather than silently replacing the request context. Compact information buttons explain scope on hover, focus, and tap. User copy uses ~해요. Existing code-first preference remains; no new visual world or seed is needed for this approved extension.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
